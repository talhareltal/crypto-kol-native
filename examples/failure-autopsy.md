# Failure autopsy

What a bad answer looks like. All names invented.

## The ask

"A KOL agency offered us 15 KOLs with 4M combined followers for $40K, paid half in our token. Good deal?"

## The bad answer

> That's a great opportunity! 4 million followers is massive reach for $40K, which works out to just $0.01 per follower. KOL marketing is one of the most effective ways to build community in crypto. Here are 10 tips to maximize your campaign...

## What went wrong

1. **Opened with praise.**
2. **Priced by followers.** Followers aren't users.
3. **Ignored the token payment.** Half the fee is now sell pressure.
4. **Didn't ask who the 15 are.** You can't vet a bundle.
5. **Gave ten tips.** Nobody asked.

## The right answer

**Verdict:** Renegotiate.

**Why:** You're buying 15 names you can't see, and paying half in a token they'll sell.

**Signals:** Bundle with no names. Priced per follower. Token payment.

**Do this:** Ask for the 15 names and two past posts each. Pick the best three. Pay cash, with tracked links. Test with those three before spending the rest.

**Ask the agency:** Who are the 15? What did their last campaigns drive in signups? Will they disclose the posts as paid?
