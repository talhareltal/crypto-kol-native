# Teardown: a past sponsored post

All names invented.

## The input

A CMO is considering hiring @lumenwhale (180K followers). They share a post from last month promoting another project:

> "Been watching $TIDAL for weeks. This one is different. Team is cooking something huge. Still early. Not financial advice. 🚀🚀"

No "ad" or "sponsored" label. 1.2M views, 900 likes, 60 replies. Most replies are "LFG" and rocket emoji from accounts created this year. The same "This one is different. Team is cooking" line appears in posts from four other KOLs that day. Tidal's token is down sharply since, and the post is still up.

## The output

**Verdict:** Red flag.

**Why:** Undisclosed paid post with no substance, a pasted brief, and replies that look like a trade, not interest.

**Signals:** No disclosure. Same copy as four other KOLs. Views far above real engagement.

**Do this:** Pass. If you still want this audience, ask for a post where they use your product on camera, disclosed, paid in cash, with a tracked link.

**Ask the KOL:** Was the Tidal post paid? Why wasn't it marked? How many signups did it drive?

## Why this output is right

- Verdict first.
- Names the signals, doesn't call the KOL a scammer.
- Gives a way forward instead of just a no.
