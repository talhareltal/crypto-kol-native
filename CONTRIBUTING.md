# Contributing

PRs welcome. Same rules as the skill: short, direct, no fluff.

## Hard rules

- **No real KOL or project names in examples.** Invent them. PRs that name a real person or project get sent back.
- **No accusations.** Describe signals, never call anyone fake, botted or a scammer.
- **No price talk.** Nothing that helps plan pumps, targets or coordinated buying.
- **No em dashes.**
- **Every new red flag needs a fix.** Say what to do instead.

## Good contributions

- A vetting signal you check every time
- A deal term that saved you money
- A post teardown (invented names)
- A failure autopsy: a bad AI answer and why it's bad

## KOLs

If you're a KOL and think something here is unfair to people doing it properly, open an issue. Good KOLs should come out of this skill looking better.
