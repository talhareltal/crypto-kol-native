# Measuring a KOL campaign

Did it work, or did it just trend for an afternoon?

## Set up before you pay

- One tracked link or referral code per KOL
- Know your baseline: signups, wallets, deposits on a normal week
- Decide what success means before the campaign, not after

## Metrics that mean something

- New users or wallets per KOL, from their link
- How many were still active after 7 and 30 days
- Deposits, trades or actions that matter to your business
- Cost per retained user, compared to your other channels
- Real questions in replies and in your community afterwards

## Metrics that mean little

- Impressions and views
- Likes
- Follower growth on your account (often other KOLs and bots)
- Short-term token price or volume
- "Mindshare" scores

## Reading the result

**Worked:** retained users at a cost you'd pay again.
**Unclear:** spike in traffic, no tracking, can't tell who stayed. Fix tracking, test once more, small.
**Didn't work:** users came and left, or never came. Stop. Don't double down to "build momentum".

## Questions to ask the KOL or agency

1. How many clicks and signups came from your link?
2. What happened in your replies and DMs?
3. Would you post about us again without being paid?

The last one tells you a lot.
