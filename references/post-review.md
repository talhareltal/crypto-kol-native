# Reviewing a past sponsored post

The best preview of what a KOL will do for you is what they did for someone else. Ask for two or three past sponsored posts, or find them yourself.

## First: is it even a paid post?

Unlabelled posts are often still paid. Signs:
- Reads like a brief: feature lists and company phrasing, not the KOL's own opinion
- Quote-posts or reposts the brand's launch the same day, with a link
- Bio says "DM for collabs", "promo", or lists a business email
- A call to action ("check it out", "explore it") with a link to the product
- Same wording as other accounts that day

You can't prove it from the outside. Say "likely paid" and ask the KOL directly.

## Seven checks

**1. Disclosed?** Is it clearly marked as paid (ad, sponsored, paid partnership)? If not, that's a legal risk for the brand, not just the KOL.

**2. Substance.** Does the post say what the product does and why it matters? Or is it a ticker, a rocket and "don't fade this"?

**3. Their own voice.** Does it sound like the KOL wrote it, with their opinion and experience? Search a sentence from it. If several KOLs posted the same words the same day, it was a pasted brief.

**4. Did they use it?** Screenshots, a walkthrough, a real result. "I tried it and here's what happened" beats "check this out".

**5. Fit.** Does this KOL normally talk about this category? Does the post make sense next to the rest of their feed?

**6. Replies.** Real questions about the product mean real interest. Bot replies, or replies only about price, mean the audience read it as a trade.

**7. Aftermath.** What happened after 7 and 30 days? Is the project still active? Did the KOL keep talking about it, or move on the next day? Is the post still up?

## Red flags

- Price targets, "100x", "last chance", "guaranteed"
- "Not financial advice" doing all the legal work
- No disclosure on an obvious paid post
- Same copy as other KOLs
- Post deleted later
- Promoted a direct competitor the week before or after

## Verdict

**Good post:** disclosed, real substance, their own voice, used the product, audience asked real questions, project still alive.

**Weak:** disclosed and harmless but generic. Pasted brief, no product use, likes but no real replies. You'd be paying for a billboard.

**Red flag:** undisclosed, price talk, bot replies, deleted, or the project collapsed and the KOL went quiet.

## Output

**Verdict:** Good post / Weak / Red flag
**Why:** one or two sentences.
**Signals:** the three that mattered.
**Ask the KOL:** what to ask before hiring them.
