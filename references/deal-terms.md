# Deal terms

Most KOL deals fail on terms, not on the KOL.

## Pay

- **Cash over tokens.** Paying in tokens pays them to sell your token, often into your own community.
- **If you must pay in tokens:** long vesting, no unlock around the campaign, disclosure that they hold it.
- **Milestones over upfront.** Part on posting, part on results you can track.
- **Know the market rate.** Ask two or three comparable KOLs. Quotes vary wildly.

## Deliverables

- Exactly what gets posted, where, how many times, and when
- Whether the post stays up (minimum time live)
- Whether they'll answer replies and questions
- Whether they actually use the product on camera or in screenshots

## Content

- **You review for facts, they keep their voice.** Fully scripted posts read as ads and underperform.
- **No price talk** in the contract. No targets, no "buy before".
- **Disclosure required** in the contract, in the format your markets require.
- **No competitor promos** for an agreed window, if it matters.

## Protection

- Kill clause if they post something that damages you
- Right to ask for removal if facts change
- No payment for engagement you can show was bought

## Red flags in the proposal

- Price is per follower count, with no audience data
- "Guaranteed reach" or "guaranteed pump"
- Bundled packages of 20 KOLs you can't vet individually
- Agency won't name the KOLs before payment
- Paid in your token at a discount, with no vesting

## Always

Token promotion has legal rules in many markets, and they change. Get counsel to review the contract and disclosure wording.
